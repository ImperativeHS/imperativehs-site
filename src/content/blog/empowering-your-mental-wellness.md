Mental health impacts every aspect of our lives. From our relationships and work performance to our overall well-being, our mental health plays a pivotal role. Yet, mental health issues remain highly stigmatized in society. Many struggle silently, overwhelmed by feelings of shame or fear of judgment.

At Imperative Healthcare Services (IHS), we envision a community where mental health is prioritized, stigma is eliminated, and all individuals can freely and confidently seek the resources they need to thrive. We are dedicated to making this vision a reality.

## Who We Are

IHS is a full-service outpatient mental health clinic situated right here in Maryland. Our team of licensed psychiatrists, psychologists, therapists, and counselors has over 50 years of combined experience providing compassionate, comprehensive mental healthcare.

## Our Mission and Vision

Our mission is to promote mental wellness and empower individuals to live fulfilling, meaningful lives. We do this by offering personalized evidence-based mental health services tailored to each client’s unique needs and goals.

Our vision is a community where mental healthcare is seamlessly integrated into regular health management. We envision a destigmatized society where seeking mental health support is as commonplace as seeing your primary care provider or dentist.

At IHS, we strive each day to make our vision of inclusive, accessible, comprehensive mental healthcare a reality. We walk hand-in-hand with each client on their personal wellness journey, providing personalized guidance and support.

## Compassionate, Evidence-Based Care

Mental health issues manifest uniquely in each individual. The path to wellness varies based on your specific symptoms, experiences, lifestyle, and preferences.

At IHS, we begin each therapeutic relationship by conducting thorough assessments to identify our client’s unique strengths, challenges, and goals. We then develop tailored treatment plans leveraging evidence-based therapeutic approaches personalized to each individual.

Whether you are struggling with anxiety, depression, PTSD, addiction, trauma, life transitions, parenting difficulties, relationship issues, or any other mental health challenge, our compassionate team will meet you where you are. We proudly offer judgment-free support to empower you to take control of your mental health.

**Services Tailored to Your Needs**

IHS delivers comprehensive outpatient mental health services spanning assessment, medication management, therapy, counseling, rehabilitation, and more. Our services include:

**Psychiatric Evaluation**

Initial appointments with our psychiatrists involve thorough assessments of symptoms, health history, experiences, goals, and more. Our psychiatrists then offer diagnoses if appropriate and work collaboratively to develop effective treatment plans. They also provide referrals to therapy and other IHS services as needed.

**Mental Health Assessment**

Our licensed psychologists and therapists perform comprehensive mental health assessments, evaluating clients’ symptoms, behaviors, strengths, challenges, and goals. Assessments inform personalized treatment plans.

**Medication Management**

Our experienced psychiatrists provide ongoing medication management services including consultation, prescription, monitoring, and dosage adjustments. We ensure medications align with treatment goals.

**Individual & Group Psychotherapy**

Our skilled therapists offer research-backed therapeutic techniques including cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), dialectical behavior therapy (DBT), and mindfulness-based therapies tailored to each client’s needs. Both individual and group sessions are available.

**Family & Couples Counseling**

IHS family therapists provide specialized relationship counseling for couples and families coping with mental health difficulties. We build communication skills, offer coping strategies for challenges, and foster healthy family dynamics.

 **Counseling for Parents**

Our family therapists offer parenting-specific counseling, support, and skill-building. We provide strategies for positive discipline, emotional regulation, confidence building, addressing parenting stress, and more.

**Children & Adolescent Therapy**

Specialized age-appropriate counseling, therapy, and support groups help young people develop healthy coping strategies and process complex issues like grief, life transitions, trauma, emotional dysregulation, and more.

**Psychiatric Rehabilitation Programs (PRP)**

IHS offers comprehensive Psychiatric Rehabilitation Programs including case management, community integration training, social skill building, vocational rehab, independent living skills training, and more for adolescents and adults. OurPRP helps individuals achieve personal goals and live fulfilling independent lives.
