## A judgment-free place to start

Reaching out for help takes courage. From your first call, our team works to make you feel welcome, heard, and respected. We start with a thorough assessment of your needs and goals, then build a treatment plan around you — whether that includes therapy, medication, or both.

## Conditions we treat

We provide treatment for a wide range of psychiatric and psychological conditions, including:

- Depression and anxiety
- Trauma, grief, and loss
- Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
- Life transitions and postpartum depression
- Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)
- Bipolar disorder and schizophrenia
- Mood disorders and anger management
- School-related crises
- Effects of abuse

Don’t see your concern listed? Give us a call — we’re happy to talk through whether we’re the right fit.

## How treatment works

1. **Reach out.** Call us or request an appointment online. You can also take our free online screening first.
2. **Assessment.** A licensed clinician or psychiatric provider meets with you to understand your history, symptoms, strengths, and goals.
3. **Your plan.** Together, we create a personalized treatment plan — therapy, medication management, or a combination.
4. **Ongoing support.** We check in regularly and adjust your plan as your needs change.
