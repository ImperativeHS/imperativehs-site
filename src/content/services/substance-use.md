## Recovery is possible

Substance use is often a response to deeper pain, trauma, and life challenges. Our Substance Use Disorder (SUD) program provides compassionate, evidence-based support to help people reclaim their lives and build healthier futures.

Substance use disorder is a medical condition that affects the brain and behavior, making it difficult to control the use of alcohol, prescription medications, or other drugs. Left untreated, it can affect physical health, mental well-being, relationships, and daily life — but with the right support, people recover every day.

## Our approach

We use a holistic, person-centered, and trauma-informed approach that meets you where you are. Our services include:

- Clinical assessments and individualized treatment planning
- Individual and group therapy, in person or virtual
- Treatment for co-occurring mental health conditions (dual diagnosis)
- Education on addiction, relapse prevention, and coping skills
- Peer recovery coaching and help navigating community support
- Family support and counseling
- Linkage to medication-assisted treatment (MAT) when needed
- Case management and resource coordination
