## Rebuilding lives, restoring hope

Our Psychiatric Rehabilitation Program (PRP) promotes recovery, full community integration, and a better quality of life for people living with a serious mental health condition. Recovery looks different for everyone, so our services are designed to meet you wherever you are.

Our licensed clinicians work with you to identify your strengths, set achievable goals, and celebrate every milestone on the path to independence.

## Comprehensive support services

- **Skills development** — daily living skills, social interaction, and workplace readiness
- **Mental health support** — symptom and illness management, stress management, and crisis intervention
- **Vocational support** — job readiness, training, and help with workplace accommodations
- **Housing assistance** — finding and keeping stable, independent housing
- **Peer support** — connecting with others who share similar experiences
- **Case management** — coordinating services and advocating for continuity of care

## Program activities

Examples of the services offered through our community PRP include:

- Budgeting and personal living skills
- Anger management and relaxation techniques
- Computer and technology training
- Assistance with entitlements (Social Security, mobility)
- Physical health and wellness
- Co-occurring substance use and mental health support
- Art and creative expression
- Social skills development and empowerment
- Personal safety and self-advocacy
